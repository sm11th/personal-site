Currently Reading:

  • Travels in Hyperreality by Umberto Eco - a very fun collection of Eco's newspaper columns in the 60s and 70s. I haven't read that much of Eco but I did read and appreciate Ur-Fascism. I picked this book up for 50 HKD at the bookstore Book Punch in Prince Edward, which is a really fantastic community space and I want to give them all my money.
  • The Meek One by Dostoevsky - pretty good short story
  • Normal People by Sally Rooney - I have a lot of thoughts about this book but it just boils down to me thinking, 'this is very mid'. I'm not even mad about the 'controversial' parts and I don't care at all that Sally Rooney is a Marxist, I just thought that some things felt shoehorned in and the time-jump format was weak and distracting. The tell-not-show approach was also not my style. Protagonists' internal monologues on things like 'culture as class performance' seemed unsubtly like authorial asides, which took away from their impact. It's like Rooney doesn't trust her readers to understand that this book is actually about class divides, so she has to overtly say 'there are conflicts due to class divides going on here', just like how she doesn't trust us to understand that a character is having a mental health episode, so she explicitly says 'Connell was having a lot of panic attacks these days' after describing the symptoms of a panic attack.
